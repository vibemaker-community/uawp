# UAWP Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good faith while addressing the actual technical or community impact.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect private information and security reports.
- Respect project scope, licensing, and maintainer decisions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption,
publication of another person's private information, and unwanted sexual
attention are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through
<https://github.com/vibemaker-community/uawp/security/advisories/new>. Do not
put sensitive reports in public issues. Li Rui is responsible for fair and
proportionate enforcement and may edit, reject, or remove contributions or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Good-faith reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation
against a reporter is prohibited.
